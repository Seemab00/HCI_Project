# SpiceSync - Fast Food Management System

## 📌 About

**SpiceSync** is a culturally tailored, AI-driven fast food management system designed for Pakistan's urban lifestyle. It bridges the gap between personalization and cultural authenticity by offering AI-curated meal combos, real-time order tracking, regional spice customization, and eco-friendly delivery options.

---

## ✨ Features

### Customer
- Social media login (Google/Facebook)
- AI-curated meal recommendations (spice levels, vegetarian, etc.)
- Real-time order tracking
- Regional spice customization (Peshawari, Punjabi, Islamabad mild)
- Subscription plans for weekly office lunches
- Halal certification & ingredient transparency
- Multiple payment options (JazzCash, Easypaisa, COD)
- Recipe contests ("Design your burger")
- Loyalty reward points (Spice Points)
- Multilingual support (Urdu/English)
- Nutritional information & calorie counter
- Instant feedback rating system
- Group ordering with bulk discounts
- Festive promotions (Eid, Ramadan, Independence Day)
- Integration with Foodpanda/Cheetah
- Voice-assisted ordering via "SpiceBot"

### Admin
- Customer management
- Catalog management
- Order management
- Financial reporting
- Promotions & discounts control
- Delivery management
